Ongoing Partnership

Data & AI Expertise, Without the Full-Time Hire

Most organisations need data and AI skills every month, but not always enough to justify full-time specialists. Dashboards need updating, systems need looking after, questions need answering, and AI opportunities need to be built, tested and improved.

As your Data & AI Partner, RK Consulting becomes your go-to data and AI expert. We get to know your business, your systems and your priorities, then keep everything running, improve it over time, and build new data and AI tools when the opportunity is right.

What's Included

One Partner, Data & AI Covered

Support is shaped around your priorities and can shift between these areas as your needs change.

Dashboards & Reporting

Maintain and improve your Power BI and Looker Studio dashboards, keep regular reporting packs running, and build new reports as your questions change.

Systems & Automation Upkeep

Look after your Airtable bases, spreadsheets, automations and Skyvia data pipelines and backups: fixing issues, adding enhancements and building new workflows as your processes evolve.

AI Workflows & Tools

Build, run and improve AI-powered workflows and tools for your team, such as document extraction, email triage, AI in Airtable and Sheets, and internal assistants, as models and needs change.

Ad-hoc Analysis & Advice

Answer business questions with data, run one-off analysis for decisions and board papers, and be the go-to person when data questions come up.

AI & Data Strategy

Ongoing AI and data advice: spotting the next opportunities, keeping your roadmap current, choosing the right tools, and keeping data quality and AI use responsible.

Training & Enablement

Help your team get more from the tools you have, from building their own reports to using AI tools confidently and safely day to day.
