Your Operational Hub, Now Intelligent

Airtable is where many teams already run their operations: clients, projects, events, requests and assets. Much of the work around it is still manual, such as reading attachments, sorting enquiries, writing summaries and replying to people.

RK Consulting combines Airtable with AI models like Claude, GPT and Gemini, and with Airtable's own AI features, so that work happens automatically as records arrive. Your team reviews the results in a clear Airtable interface instead of doing the work by hand.
