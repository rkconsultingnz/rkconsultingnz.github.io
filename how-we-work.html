Our Process

Six Steps, No Surprises

Every engagement follows the same simple path, whether it's a single dashboard or a complete operational system.

  1. 01

    Free First Conversation

    We talk through your challenge, your goals and the tools you use today. There's no cost and no obligation.

  2. 02

    Discovery & Scoping

    We look at your current data, processes and pain points, and agree what success looks like for your business.

  3. 03

    Clear Proposal

    You receive a scoped proposal with the deliverables, approach and timeline set out before any work begins.

  4. 04

    Build in Stages

    We deliver in manageable stages with regular check-ins, so you see progress early and can shape the result as it develops.

  5. 05

    Test & Refine

    We test with real data and real users, and refine until the solution is reliable and fits the way your team works.

  6. 06

    Training & Ongoing Support

    We can provide tailored training so your team is confident from day one, with optional ongoing support as your needs grow.
