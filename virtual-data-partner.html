Ongoing Support

Data & AI Expertise, Without the Full-Time Hire

Most organisations need data and AI skills every month, but not always enough to justify a full-time specialist. Dashboards need updating, systems need looking after, questions need answering and new ideas need testing.

As your Virtual Data Partner, RK Consulting becomes your go-to data and AI expert. We get to know your business, your systems and your priorities, then provide ongoing support that keeps everything running and improving.

What's Included

One Partner, Every Data Need

Support is shaped around your priorities and can shift between these areas as your needs change.

Dashboards & Reporting

Maintain and improve your Power BI and Looker Studio dashboards, keep regular reporting packs running, and build new reports as your questions change.

Systems & Automation Upkeep

Look after your Airtable bases, spreadsheets and automations: fixing issues, adding enhancements and building new workflows as your processes evolve.

Ad-hoc Analysis & Advice

Answer business questions with data, run one-off analysis for decisions and board papers, and be the go-to person when data questions come up.

AI & Data Strategy

Ongoing AI advice and roadmap, data quality and governance, tool selection, and training so your team gets more from the tools you already have.
