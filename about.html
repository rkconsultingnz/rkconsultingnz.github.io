The Founder

Practical, outcome-focused data and AI

Rahul is a data and AI leader with more than 15 years of experience helping organisations turn complex data into clear insights, smarter decisions and measurable value.

He founded RK Consulting to bring that experience to SMEs, not-for-profits and public sector organisations. Combining deep expertise in data, AI and automations, he helps teams simplify processes, work smarter and unlock new opportunities for growth. His approach is practical and outcome-focused: understand the business challenge first, then deliver solutions that make a real difference.

Alongside his consulting work, Rahul lectures in business intelligence and Generative AI and mentors emerging data professionals, sharing what he's learned with the next generation.

His mission is simple: turn data into decisions, and AI into advantage.
